Frequently asked questions

Do you really buy damaged bags too?

Yes — that's our specialty. Scuffs, stains, faded colour and damaged leather are fine; after restoration we resell the piece.

How fast do I get an offer?

Within 24 hours of sending photos and basic information.

How do you verify authenticity?

Professionally — via Hermès specialists and verification tools. For damaged/vintage pieces it's standard and protects both sides.

How does payment work?

After agreement and personal handover (or insured shipping) we pay immediately, with a purchase contract. Cash within legal limits, otherwise by transfer.

Is it discreet?

Absolutely. We don't share your data and the dealing is confidential.
